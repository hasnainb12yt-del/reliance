## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end, serene hospitality inspired by the stark, majestic mountain landscapes of Quetta and the heritage of Pakistani hospitality, balanced with the restrained minimalism of Aman, Four Seasons, and The Serena. 

The aesthetic is grounded in architectural minimalism paired with tactile editorial luxury. It evokes calm, exclusivity, permanence, and refined warmth. The interface utilizes generous negative space, intentional typography pairings, subtle hairline borders, and deep, atmospheric tonal contrasts. 

Key Brand Tenets:
- **Quiet Dignity:** Visuals never shout. Elements breathe through disciplined whitespace, measured proportions, and unhurried visual rhythm.
- **Architectural Precision:** Clean grid structures, sharp structural lines, and delicate hairline dividers (0.5px to 1px) reference modern mountain pavilions and classical caravanserai architecture.
- **Subtle Luminescence:** Champagne and muted golds are treated as delicate highlights—accents, thin borders, fine metadata labels—never as heavy, gaudy fills.

## Colors

The palette employs a bi-modal architecture: an immersive, evening-inspired dark canvas for brand heroics, suite showcases, and evening dining, alongside a crisp, sunlit light canvas for transactional flows, daylight dining, and administrative interfaces.

### Dark Palette (Primary / Default)
- **Obsidian Black (`#080A0F`):** Deep canvas background.
- **Deep Navy Blue (`#0B1628`):** Elevated structural background and headers.
- **Deep Navy Card Surface (`#111F36`):** Card containers, dialogs, and elevated panels.
- **Border Subdued (`rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.18)`):** Hairline borders on dark surfaces.

### Light Palette (Alternate / Daytime Booking)
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity booking surface.
- **Warm Light Gray (`#F4F5F7`):** Secondary container fills and table rows.
- **Charcoal (`#1E2229`):** Primary body and headline text.
- **Slate (`#555E6D`):** Secondary descriptions and supporting metadata.
- **Border Light (`rgba(85, 94, 109, 0.15)`):** Subtle dividing lines on bright surfaces.

### Accent & Metallic Tokens
- **Champagne Gold (`#C8A96B`):** Primary focal accents, active states, key interactive indicators, and primary gold badges.
- **Soft Muted Gold (`#DFCA9B`):** Hover states, decorative rules, and secondary badges.
- **Warm Light Gold Accent (`#F3E8CE`):** Soft luminous highlights, text on muted gold tags, and subtle chip backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographical pairing contrasts classical grandeur with contemporary functional clarity.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys heritage, poise, and world-class stature. Headings preserve natural tracking with slight negative adjustments at grand display scales to emphasize sculptural letterforms.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers immaculate clarity across booking engines, service itineraries, and room specifications.
- **Labels & Overlines (Plus Jakarta Sans Uppercase):** All `label-*` tiers are presented in uppercase with deliberate letter spacing (`0.12em` to `0.18em`). Used exclusively for categories, amenity tags, room status, dates, and currency prefixes.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous outer canvases:
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 4rem outer page margins. Section paddings span a generous 5rem to 7.5rem vertical rhythm.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, and 2.5rem page margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, and 1.25rem page margins.

Vertical density is intentionally relaxed to simulate the calm expanse of luxury retreat architecture. Grouped form controls (such as the booking bar) leverage internal micro-spacers (`space-sm` to `space-md`), whereas editorial content clusters deploy expansive breathing space (`space-xl` and above).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows; rather, it uses refined tonal layering paired with 1px luminous hairline borders.

- **Base Layer:** Obsidian Black (`#080A0F`) serves as the foundational floor.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Cards):** Deep Navy Card Surface (`#111F36`) bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.16)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Modals & Popovers):** Pure Deep Navy (`#0B1628`) framed by `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.35)` with an ambient diffused shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Light Surface Depth:** Pure white cards placed against `#F4F5F7` with razor-thin borders (`rgba(85, 94, 109, 0.12)`) and light ambient diffusion: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(11, 22, 40, 0.05)`.
- **Image Overlays:** Dark linear gradients (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(8,10,15,0) 40%, rgba(8,10,15,0.85) 100%)`) ground photography and elevate overlaid typography.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy adopts a sharp, architectural standard (`0`). 

Right angles across cards, booking modules, buttons, and media containers evoke bespoke stonework, high-end stationery, and quiet architectural presence. Form fields and badges maintain crisp, clean corners. When soft circular tags or round icons are strictly required for universal recognition (such as a circular date indicator or avatar), they remain isolated exceptions while all bounding containers enforce unrounded, crisp edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Champagne Gold (`#C8A96B`) background, Obsidian Black (`#080A0F`) text, `label-lg` uppercase typography, 0px border radius, with `16px 32px` padding. Transitions on hover to Soft Muted Gold (`#DFCA9B`).
- **Secondary (Outline):** 1px border in `#C8A96B`, transparent background, Champagne Gold text. Transitions on hover to a subtle fill `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary (Text/Editorial):** Champagne Gold text followed by a fine 1px horizontal rule underline with `8px` offset, transitioning to an extended underline on hover.

### Horizontal Booking Search Bar
- A monolithic floating bar spanning the grid with a Deep Navy (`#0B1628`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) background.
- Divided into four columns: Destination / Suite Type, Check-in / Check-out, Guests, and CTA.
- Columns are demarcated by 1px vertical dividers (`rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.25)` or `rgba(85, 94, 109, 0.2)`).
- Input labels use `label-sm` in Champagne Gold or Slate, while input values appear in `body-md` in White or Charcoal.

### Cards (Suites & Dining)
- Deep Navy Card Surface (`#111F36`) container with a 1px border of `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.16)`.
- Aspect ratio of photography is strictly 16:10 or 4:3 with zero corner rounding.
- Hover state: Subtle image zoom (1.03 scale over 600ms ease) accompanied by border brightening to `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.4)`.
- Price display: Serif headline font (`Playfair Display`, `headline-md`) anchored with small uppercase currency labels.

### Badges & Chips
- Crisp 0px borders.
- Background: `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.1)` with 1px border in `#C8A96B`.
- Text: Soft Muted Gold (`#DFCA9B`), set in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat surfaces with zero rounding.
- Inactive state: 1px bottom border in `rgba(200, 169, 107, 0.3)` or solid slate.
- Active/Focused state: 1px continuous border in `#C8A96B` with zero outer glow ring.
- Placeholder text in Slate (`#555E6D`), transitioning seamlessly to crisp White or Charcoal when populated.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom square markers (0px radius) with a 1px border in `#C8A96B`.
- Checked state: Solid `#C8A96B` fill with an Obsidian Black check icon.
